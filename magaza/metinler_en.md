## 4. Full description (Google Play and App Store, limit 4000)

Works for both stores (about 2,130 characters).

```
Everyone is locked in a separate cell. Your only link to the outside: the red phone on the wall.
Call your friends, talk with your real voice, team up... or betray them at the worst possible moment!

Hat Düştü is a voice chat prison party game for 2-8 players. You never know who is telling the truth: scores stay hidden until the end of the match, so the winner is revealed only at the very last second.

7 WILD MINI GAMES
• Blackboard: A secret word is written on one cell's board. Find it by phone and write it on yours.
• Short Circuit: Lights out! Your wiring diagram is on someone else's board. Call them and fix your fuse box.

• Liar: Everyone has the same item in their locker, except one. Find the liar, or be the liar.
• Russian Roulette: Pull the trigger on yourself or aim at someone else? Last one standing wins.
• C4: The bomb is in your hands and nobody knows when it goes off. Get close to someone and pass it on!
• Average Number: Pick a number from 0 to 100. Closest to half the average wins.

REAL VOICES, REAL BLUFFS
• Dial any cell from the wall phone and talk with your own voice.
• Players nearby can hear you too, so close the door.
• Lying is allowed. Getting caught has a price.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS OR QUICK PLAY
• Add friends and invite them to your room with one tap.
• Create a private room with a 4-character code.
• Jump into a match with Quick Play; bots fill the empty seats.

CREATE YOUR INMATE
• Male or female inmate, skin and eye color
• 20+ hairstyles, beards and moustaches
• Hats, glasses, headphones, necklaces, tattoos and outfits
• 20 emotes and dances to show off

OPEN CHESTS
• Wooden and Gold chests drop common, rare, epic and legendary items.
• Complete missions, unlock achievements, claim your daily reward.
• Climb the weekly leaderboard.

Hat Düştü is free to play and offers optional in-app purchases (gems). Voice chat requires microphone permission; conversations are never recorded. You can report and block players from inside the game.

The phone is ringing. Will you pick up?
```

Note: the game's interface is currently Turkish only. Until the English UI ships, publish this English listing only if you add "Turkish language only" or wait for the translation; otherwise reviewers and players may complain. (See the checklist.)

## 8. Age rating — summary (details in `metinler_tr.md`, section 8)

- Cartoon violence against human-like characters (Russian Roulette with a revolver, a C4 bomb that explodes). No blood or gore.
- Note: a character can point the revolver at their own head. If the questionnaire asks about self-harm/suicide themes, answer honestly (yes, stylized game mechanic); this may raise PEGI to 16.
- Users interact via live voice chat (unfiltered, not recorded); user-chosen names; report and block available.
- In-app purchases, including random items (chests / loot boxes).
- No real-money gambling, no simulated gambling, no sexual content, no drugs, no profanity in game text, no location sharing, no web browser.

Expected: **PEGI 12**, USK 12, ESRB **Teen** (or E10+), Google Play 12+; Apple **13+** (new 2025 system).

## 9. Data safety / App Privacy — summary (details in `metinler_tr.md`, section 9)

Collected (none shared, none used for tracking, all encrypted in transit):
- Username, display name, salted password hash (PBKDF2) — Personal info: Name, User IDs / Apple: Name, User ID
- Random account key generated on the device — Play: Device or other IDs / Apple: User ID
- Purchase history (pack, price, receipt) — Financial info: Purchase history / Apple: Purchase History
- Gameplay data, friends list, blocks — App activity / Apple: Gameplay Content
- Report notes — Other user-generated content / Apple: Other User Content
- Crash reports and diagnostics (device model, OS, language), not linked to the account — App info and performance / Apple: Crash Data, Other Diagnostic Data, Product Interaction (not linked)
- Voice: streamed live only to the players you talk to, never stored — Play: Audio, marked "processed ephemerally" / Apple: not disclosed (real-time only)
- No ads today. If rewarded ads (AdMob) are added later, update both forms.
