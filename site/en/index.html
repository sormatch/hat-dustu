Rules on the board

(if somebody wipes it, we’ll write it again)

  1. Get in your cell

    4 to 8 players, each in a separate cell. Set up a room with friends, or hop into Quick Play if nobody’s around.

  2. Pick up the phone

    Use the red phone on the wall to call any cell. Give info, get info, cut a deal. Nobody said you have to tell the truth.

  3. Face the music

    You play a few mini games and the scores stay secret till the end. Then the ranking is read out from last to first, and the winner gets a crown.

Mini games

Every match deals out a few of these, and the last stop is always the Great Escape. Tap the cards. Some of them answer.

  • HD-01

    Who’s Who?

    Names are hidden and every voice sounds like a robot. Work out who’s on which line and match them up on the board.

    BEEP BOOP?
  • HD-02

    Blackboard

    One player writes a secret word. The rest get it over the phone and copy it onto their own board. Exactly the right number of people have to know it.

    SCREECH
  • HD-03

    Short Circuit

    Lights out. The diagram for your fuse box is on someone else’s board. Call them, get talked through it, fix it together.

    ZAP!
  • HD-04

    Liar

    Everyone has the same item in their locker. Except one person, who isn’t going to mention it. Find them and vote.

    LIAR!
  • HD-05

    Russian Roulette

    One revolver on the table. Point it at yourself or at someone else. Last one left at the table wins.

    CLICK.
  • HD-06

    C4

    A bomb goes from hand to hand and nobody knows when it’ll blow. The doors are open. Run over and pass it on.

    BOOM!
  • HD-07

    Average Number

    Write down a number. Whoever is closest to half the average wins. Looks like maths. It’s really mind reading.

    23?
  • HD-08

    Searchlight Hunt

    Night in the yard, searchlights on the towers. Hide behind cover and move when the dog comes sniffing. Get caught and you watch in silence.

    WOOF!

Final game of every match

Great Escape

There are tools hidden around your cell. Find them, follow the recipes in your notebook, get to work.

  1. Make a shovel, a flashlight and a lockpick.
  2. Unscrew the toilet with your pocket knife and dig underneath.
  3. Get past the concrete foundation and the wire fence.
  4. At head count, be in your cell or leave a dummy in your bed.

The earlier you get out, the more points you get. Hurry. Don’t get caught.

News from the cell

Mind the cord

The phone on the wall really has a cord. Wander too far mid-call and the line drops. That’s the name, by the way: Hat Düştü is Turkish for “the line dropped”.

Proximity voice chat: people next to you sound clear, people down the corridor sound faint. Sound doesn’t get through closed doors, so shut yours before you whisper.

secret plan = closed door

Locked up, still stylish

Skin, hair, beard, hats, accessories, outfits, emotes. Propeller beanie, rubber duck, Viking helmet. The guards haven’t said a word.

Crack crates, fill your locker

Whatever drops from a crate goes into your inventory, and you wear what you like. There are daily tasks, a level road and a weekly leaderboard too. No sitting around in this cell.

Ranked bottom up

Nobody knows their score during the match. At the end names are revealed from the bottom, so last place finds out first. The last name read out gets the crown.

coming last is something. not much, but something.

Block rules

  1. Add friends, set up a room. If nobody’s around, there’s Quick Play.
  2. Swear words go through a filter.
  3. If someone’s being a pain, report or block them. You won’t hear players you’ve blocked.
