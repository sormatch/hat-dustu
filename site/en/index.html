How to play

One simple rule: nobody can see anyone. You can only hear them.

1

Enter your cell

4 to 8 players, each in a separate cell. Create a room with friends or jump straight in with Quick Play.

2

Grab the phone

Call someone from the wall phone. Share secrets, bargain, persuade… or just lie.

3

Take the crown

Scores stay hidden until the very end. After 7 games the ranking is revealed from last to first and the winner is crowned.

7 wild mini games

Every match is different. In every game, decide again who to trust.

Who's Who?

Voices are robotic, the lines are scrambled. Figure out who is behind each line.

Blackboard

Someone knows the secret word. Find out by phone and write it on your board.

Short Circuit

Lights out! The diagram for your fuse box is on someone else's board.

Liar

Everyone has an item in their locker. Someone is lying. Vote!

Russian Roulette

A revolver goes around the table. Shoot yourself, or someone else?

C4

The bomb is in your hands and the clock is ticking. Pass it on and shut the door!

Average Number

Write a number from 0 to 100. Closest to half of the average wins.

Features

🎙️

Realistic voice chat

Hear nearby players clearly and distant ones faintly. Sound doesn't pass through closed doors.

☎️

Corded phone

Lift the handset and stretch the cord. Walk too far and the line drops!

👥

Play with friends

Add friends, invite them to your room, or meet new people in Quick Play.

🎁

Crates and inventory

Open crates, collect rare and legendary items, wear them from your inventory.

🧑‍🎤

Create your character

Female or male; skin, hair, beard, hats, glasses, outfits and dozens of emotes.

🏆

Missions and leaderboard

Daily rewards, missions, achievements and a weekly leaderboard.

🛡️

Safe community

Profanity filter, reporting and blocking. You never hear players you block.

🌍

English and Turkish

The game opens in your phone's language; switch anytime in settings.
